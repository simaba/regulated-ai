# AI Risk Register

Use one row per material risk. Keep evidence links and detailed incident material in the appropriate access-controlled system; this template should not become a public repository for sensitive operational data.

| Risk ID | Risk statement | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Inherent tier | Controls / mitigation | Evidence | Owner | Status | Residual decision | Review date |

| R-001 | [What could go wrong and why] | safety / privacy / security / fairness / reliability / misuse | low / medium / high | low / medium / high | low / medium / high | [Control or mitigation] | [Artifact or link reference] | [Role] | open / mitigating / accepted / closed | [Decision and accountable owner] | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Review questions

- What intended use, data, tool, model, or user change could invalidate this assessment?
- What observable signal would show the risk is occurring?
- What action is required if that signal crosses a defined threshold?
- Who may accept the residual risk, and for how long?
